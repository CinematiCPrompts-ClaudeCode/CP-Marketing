# Marketing Metrics — Glossary

Definitions the agent (and you) should use consistently. Some aren't in play yet
(no paid spend), but they're here so the system is ready the day you run ads.

## In use now (organic)
- **Reach** — unique people who saw a post at least once.
- **Impressions** — total times a post was shown (one person can rack up several).
- **Views** — video plays. Platform definitions differ (TikTok counts fast; YT is
  stricter). Always note the platform.
- **Engagement** — likes + comments + shares + saves.
- **Engagement Rate (ER)** — engagement ÷ reach (or ÷ views), as a %.
- **Watch-through / Avg. watch time** — how far people get before dropping. The
  single biggest driver of short-form distribution.
- **Follower Growth** — net new followers in the period.
- **Saves / Shares** — strongest organic signals; weight them above likes.

## The conversion chain (what actually matters)
- **App Store Taps (Outbound clicks)** — taps on your link/bio toward the store.
- **CTR (Click-Through Rate)** — taps ÷ views (or ÷ impressions), as a %.
- **Store Conversion Rate** — installs ÷ store page views.
- **Installs / Downloads** — the real goal.
- **Install-per-view** — installs ÷ views. The agent ranks content by THIS, not
  raw views.

## Ready for when you run paid (not used yet)
- **Spend** — money put into ads in the period.
- **CPM (Cost per Mille)** — cost per 1,000 impressions.
- **CPC (Cost per Click)** — cost per outbound click.
- **CPI (Cost per Install)** — spend ÷ paid installs. The headline paid number.
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)** — full cost to acquire one user (broader
  than CPI; includes everything spent, not just ad cost).
- **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)** — revenue from ads ÷ ad spend. Above 1.0 = the
  ads make money. For a paid app, revenue = installs × price (× any IAP).
- **LTV (Lifetime Value)** — total revenue one user generates over their lifetime.
- **LTV:CAC ratio** — health check; 3:1 is the common "healthy" benchmark.
- **Payback period** — how long until a user's revenue covers their CAC.

## A note on view vs. install
A post can win on views and lose on installs. When that happens it's almost
always a hook/CTA mismatch: the hook pulled the wrong audience, or the CTA was
too soft. The agent flags these explicitly.

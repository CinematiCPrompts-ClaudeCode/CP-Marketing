# Storyboard + Design-Project Brief — "The Handoff"
**Short film · Prenzlauer Berg (90s East Berlin) · TikTok-led hero piece**

Style locked: **Prenzlauer Berg** — *Gründerzeit Altbau* (ornate tenements, tall stucco facades, peeling paint, soot-stained, tall windows, carved wooden stairwell banisters, Hinterhof courtyards, cobblestones, Trabant/Wartburg cars, sodium-yellow streetlamp pools, grainy emulsion, desaturated earth tones with isolated red accents). Setting: **late-1980s East Berlin (GDR, pre-Wall)** — per the app's style spec. NOT post-Wall: no capitalist neon/new cafés. The tension is Stasi-era surveillance.

Format: 6 frames · ~48s · 9:16 vertical · 24fps (film cadence).

---

## The arc
Threat → held breath → release + curiosity. In late-1980s East Berlin a woman is hunted across a Gründerzeit courtyard at **dawn**, hiding an envelope; cornered in an ornate stairwell, she turns to face her pursuer and the expected betrayal flips into a silent, charged exchange — a clandestine handoff under a watching regime; she walks out into the **blue hour** as the sodium streetlamps glow, free but never safe. The dawn→blue-hour relight of the *same courtyard* (frames 2 & 5) carries the emotional turn — that bookend is the money shot.

## CLAUDE DESIGN PROJECT — assembly instructions

**Canvas:** 1080×1920 (9:16), 24fps, total ~48s. Safe margins: keep text inside the center 90% (TikTok UI eats edges).

**Timeline (drop clips on these in/out marks):**
- 0.0–3.0 frame 1 · 3.0–9.0 frame 2 · 9.0–18.0 frame 3 (sub-cuts 3a→3b→corner) · 18.0–30.0 frame 4 · 30.0–40.0 frame 5 · 40.0–48.0 frame 6.

**On-screen text (use the design kit — apply YOUR kit fonts + colors, not defaults):**
- `Berlin, 1992` — frame 1, fades in ~2.0s, lower third, small. Kit *display* font.
- `…you came.` — frame 4, ~1s, subtitle style, center-low. Kit *body* font, muted.
- End card frame 6: line 1 `Prenzlauer Berg · dawn to blue hour` (kit display), line 2 `Made with Cinematic Prompts` + app icon (kit body). Keep it small and confident.
- No other text. Let images carry it. NO text in the first 2s.

**Color grade:** Two-act grade. Act 1 (0–30s): cold, desaturated, lifted blacks, heavy grain — fear. Act 2 (30–48s): richer, deep teal-blue shadows with warm neon highlights, slightly cleaner. The grade shift should feel like the relief.

**Transitions:** Hard cuts only through frames 1–3 (tightening). Frame 4 holds. Frame 5 a slow dolly-back. Frame 6 a gentle settle. No fancy wipes — this is a film.

**Audio:** Low tense pulse/drone building through 0–18s → near-silence on the turn (18–30s) → a single ambient swell at the blue-hour reveal (30–40s) → tail out under the end card. Footstep/breath SFX in the pursuit. Keep dialogue out — it's wordless.

**Export:** 1080×1920, H.264, high bitrate. Master = TikTok. (IG does NOT get the film — it gets the Mexico relight demo. FB optional.)

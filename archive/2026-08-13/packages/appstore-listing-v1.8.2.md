## Description

Turn a plain idea into a filmmaker-grade prompt you paste straight into any AI image or video generator. No prompt-engineering skills — you pick the look, the app writes the prompt, so you gamble far fewer generation credits.

PICK YOUR STYLE, YOUR LIGHT, AND YOUR ANGLE
- 12 filmmaker-inspired styles: Tokyo Noir, Mexico City, Napoli, Nature Documentary, Historical Documentary, Post-Apocalyptic, Prenzlauer Berg, 90s Indie, 70s Gritty, Black & White, In India, Seen by Alien.
- 5 daylight conditions: Dawn, Sunny, Golden Hour, Blue Hour, Night.
- 6 Camera Angles: Bird's Eye View, Centered Eye-Level, Slight High Angle, Dramatic Low Angle, Close-Up (Detail Focus), Front 3/4. Frame the same scene exactly how you want it.

MARKETING PROMPTS — FOR CREATORS, SELLERS, AND BRANDS
Brand-quality prompts for Cinematic Ads, Product Photography, and Fashion Photography, each with professional studio lighting (softbox, rim, beauty dish, hard sun, and more). NEW — 6 shoot environments to place your product in: Seamless White Sweep, Neutral Studio, Lifestyle Scene, Nature Setting, Futuristic, and Luxury Interior.

IMAGE OR VIDEO — TUNED FOR EACH
Separate prompts calibrated for static image engines and motion video.

MORE TOOLS
- Cinematic Contact Sheet and Character Turnaround Sheet.
- First/last-frame Evolve prompts.
- Camera Movement presets (beta) for motion.
- Save your favorite combinations.

WORKS WITH THE TOOLS YOU USE
Output tuned for Google Gemini (Nano Banana 2 & Veo 3), Runway Gen 4.5, Kling (Image 3 & Video 3), and Seedance (Seedream 5 & Seedance 2) — and any major generator.

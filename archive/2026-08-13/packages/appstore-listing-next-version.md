## Description (body)

**Stop guessing at prompts. Get a filmmaker-grade one, first — so you gamble far fewer generation credits.**

Cinematic Prompts turns a plain idea into a detailed, professional prompt you paste straight into any AI image or video generator. You don't need prompt-engineering skills — you pick the look, the app writes the prompt.

**Pick your style. Pick your light.**
Choose from **12 filmmaker-inspired styles** — Tokyo Noir, Mexico City, Napoli, Nature Documentary, Historical Documentary, Post-Apocalyptic, Prenzlauer Berg, 90s Indie, 70s Gritty, Black & White, In India, Seen by Alien — then set the **daylight condition**: Dawn, Sunny, Golden Hour, Blue Hour, or Night. The same scene, lit exactly the way you want it.

**Image or video — tuned for each.**
Get prompts calibrated separately for static image engines and motion video, so the result fits the tool you're using.

**NEW — Marketing Prompts.**
Beyond film: generate brand-quality prompts for **Cinematic Ads, Product Photography, and Fashion Photography**, each with professional studio lighting setups (softbox, rim light, beauty dish, hard sun, and more). For creators, sellers, and brands.

**NEW — Camera-angle presets.**
Add controlled camera movement — arcs, dolly-ins, lateral glides — for motion that actually behaves.

**Plus:** first/last-frame evolve prompts, a cinematic contact sheet, and a character turnaround sheet. Save your favorite combinations.

**Works with the tools you already use.**
Output tuned for Google Gemini (Nano Banana 2 & Veo 3), Runway Gen 4.5, Kling (Image 3 & Video 3), and Seedance (Seedream 5 & Seedance 2) — and it works with any major generator.

**Who it's for:** content creators tired of burning credits on prompts that miss · filmmakers and storyboard artists exploring AI · brands and sellers who need on-style product, fashion, and ad visuals.

Free to download.

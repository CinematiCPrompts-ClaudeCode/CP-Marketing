## ⚠️ Read before writing any variety copy
This reel's natural framing is the one that has *repeatedly failed* on TikTok. The split is sharp:

| Framing | Views |
|---|---|
| "So many looks. One app." — experiential + resolution | **768** |
| "12 Cinematic Styles" | 485 |
| "12 Cinematic Styles 5 Lighting Styles 6 Camera Angles" — inventory count | **303** |
| "Cinema-grade and marketing-grade AI prompts" — category jargon | **266** |

Counting features and naming categories both lose. The winning shape states an *experience*
("so many looks") and resolves it ("one app"). Every caption below follows that, never a count.

## CTA used this round — and the experiment attached to it
**Rung: medium, plus one new element.** Every caption above closes with
**"no account needed" / "no account"** — the on-device, no-account, no-data-collected angle from
`brand/positioning.md`, which calls it "a real selling point — use it."

**Experiment: privacy as the install trigger. Signal: if downloads in the week of Aug 24 clear 30
(vs. 19 / 29 / 21 / 25 the last four weeks), keep privacy in the CTA permanently. If they land
under 22 again, privacy is not the lever and the next test moves to the App Store page itself.**

Why this experiment exists — **the most important number this round:**

| Week | Downloads |
|---|---|
| Jul 24–30 | 25 |
| Jul 31–Aug 6 | 21 |
| Aug 7–13 | 29 |
| **Aug 14–20** (the 805-view TikTok week) | **19 — the lowest of the four** |

TikTok views tripled and downloads went *down*. That is precisely the hook/CTA mismatch
`CLAUDE.md` says to flag: views that don't become taps. Getting more views on TikTok is now a
**solved problem**; converting them is not. The framing win was real, but it did not reach the
KPI that matters, and repeating the same CTA would just buy more un-converting views.

# Content Package — Instagram return-to-form (single-subject, pain-first)

**Scope: Instagram only.** This is a deliberate single-platform package, not the usual four.
Instagram is the one channel with a proven ceiling it has fallen off — 717 / 301 / 272 earlier
this year, 6–15 now — and the point of this round is to re-run the exact shape that produced the
717, changing nothing else. Adding three more platforms would add three more variables.

---

## Demo
- **What's in the clip:** one coastal/ocean animal scene, held on a single subject, re-lit across
  the real daylight conditions. No cuts to other scenes, no montage of looks.
- **Style shown:** Nature Documentary
- **Daylight lighting shown:** dawn → Golden Hour → blue hour, same scene, same subject
- **Hook angle:** wasted credits — the pain of regenerating over and over just to fix the light

## First 2 seconds (scroll-stopper)
- **On-screen text:** `Ocean light. 9 tries.`
- **Visual beat:** the animal already fully in frame at 0.0s, sharp and centred, with the light
  visibly shifting behind it. No push-in, no fade, no title card. The relight is the motion.

---

## Instagram Reels — caption

Grounding: pain-first direct-statement open + single coastal subject, **717** — the account's
best-ever post, *"Ocean and coastal AI footage is the hardest…"*. Same subject family and same
opening shape as the 717; the same subject also produced TikTok's 805. The three posts that
built Instagram's ceiling (717 / 301 / 272) were all pain-first direct statements, and every
post since that fell to single digits was either a variety reel or a narrative-title repost.
This changes the content type back, not the caption craft — the 08-27 post proved better writing
alone does not transfer (grounded on the account's own 35, returned 7).

## CTA used this round
**Soft.** "On the App Store — free, no account" names the install without an imperative. The
account is coming off single-digit reach; a hard CTA on a post that 12 people see converts
nothing and reads as pushy. The privacy/no-account angle is also the CTA variant still under
test from the 08-24 experiment, and it costs nothing to keep running here.

---

## Deliberately NOT in this package
- **No other platform.** One variable.
- **No second upload of this asset anywhere.** Seven duplicate Facebook uploads since July 1,
  second copy losing every time — that practice stops with this round.
- **No narrative title.** "The Ride" / "Revenge" / "Car Chase" reposts scored 29–30 on this
  account against 717 for a pain-first statement on comparable footage.

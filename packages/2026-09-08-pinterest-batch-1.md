# Pinterest — batch 1 (20 pins) · 08.09.2026

**Experiment:** first Pinterest batch, drawn entirely from existing stills in
`Marketing-Examples/Reels-August/`. **Signal: 30 outbound clicks in 30 days → keep and scale;
under 10 → close the channel.** Measured on **outbound clicks only** — impressions and saves are
ignored, because outbound clicks are the only social metric that proxies App Store page views
(+0.76 against downloads, versus +0.19 for views).

**Every pin links via the `ct=pinterest` App Store campaign link.** Generate it in App Store Connect
first — without it this batch is unattributable and the test cannot be graded.

**Gate note, stated plainly:** `./check.sh` does not cover Pinterest — `brand/registry.json` has no
`pinterest` entry in `verified_hashtags` or `hashtag_count`, and Pinterest does not use hashtags
anyway (keywords in the description are what get indexed). **Claims were checked by hand** against
`brand/claims.md`: the five real lighting names only, "Golden Hour" capitalised, no first-try or
zero-waste claim, no generator named in visible copy. If this channel survives its 30 days, add a
`pinterest` block to the registry so the gate covers it.

**Why no generator credit line:** these stills came from several generators and I cannot verify
which produced which. **"Prompt written in Cinematic Prompts" is accurate and generator-agnostic** —
and it already does the job the cover's credit line does, by naming the app's output as the *prompt*
rather than the picture.

## Board: `Natural Light Reference` — the Sea Otter series (5 pins)

The flagship. **One subject, one framing, five real lights** — the product's core claim shown
rather than stated, and the shape that beat variety content everywhere it has been tested. The
coastal-otter subject also sits behind Instagram's best-ever post (717) and TikTok's 805.

Pin all five here **in order**, and cross-pin `Golden Hour` and `blue hour` to their own boards —
those two are the highest-volume light searches on the platform.

**1 — dawn** (`Stills/dawn.png`)
> **Title:** Dawn light photography — cold water, first warm sun
> **Description:** Dawn on a kelp bed: the sun still low and cold, the water holding more blue than
> gold, shadows long and soft. Same scene, same framing as the four that follow — only the light
> changed. Prompt written in Cinematic Prompts. Free on the App Store, no account.

**2 — sunny** (`Stills/sunny.png`)
> **Title:** Bright midday daylight — coastal wildlife photography
> **Description:** Sunny light on the same kelp bed: hard overhead sun, clean blue sky, high
> contrast. The least forgiving of the five, and the one most shots default to. Prompt written in
> Cinematic Prompts. Free on the App Store, no account.

**3 — Golden Hour** (`Stills/golden-hour.png`) · *also pin to `Golden Hour Photography`*
> **Title:** Golden Hour photography — low amber sun across water
> **Description:** Golden Hour on the same scene: low amber sun, long shadows through the kelp, warm
> rim light along the fur. The hour everyone waits for, chosen rather than waited for. Prompt
> written in Cinematic Prompts. Free on the App Store, no account.

**4 — blue hour** (`Stills/blue-hour.png`) · *also pin to `Blue Hour Photography`*
> **Title:** Blue hour photography — twilight over a coastal inlet
> **Description:** Blue hour on the same kelp bed: the sun gone, the sky still lit, everything
> cooling toward navy while the far shore turns to silhouette. Prompt written in Cinematic Prompts.
> Free on the App Store, no account.

**5 — night** (`Stills/night.png`)
> **Title:** Night photography — starlight and distant harbour lights
> **Description:** Night on the same scene: stars over the ridge, harbour lights on the far shore,
> the kelp reading as texture rather than colour. Fifth of five lights on one unchanged frame.
> Prompt written in Cinematic Prompts. Free on the App Store, no account.

## Order of operations

1. **Generate the `ct=pinterest` campaign link in App Store Connect.** Nothing gets pinned before
   this exists, or the batch cannot be graded.
2. Create the four boards, each named and described as a search phrase.
3. Pin the otter series first, in order — it is the strongest set and the one most likely to be
   saved onto other people's boards.
4. Then the product batch, then fashion. Spread over two weeks rather than one sitting.
5. Log outbound clicks weekly in `data/performance-log.md`. Grade at 30 days.

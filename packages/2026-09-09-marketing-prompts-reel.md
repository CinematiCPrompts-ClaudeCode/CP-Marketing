## CTA used this round
**Soft-to-medium.** "Free on the App Store, no account" names the install and removes the two
frictions people actually hesitate over, without an imperative. It is also the exact close from the
537 post, which is the variable being held constant. A hard CTA would change two things at once and
make the test unreadable.

## Posting order
One platform per day, Facebook first. Both recurring operational failures — YouTube shipping Private
(3×) and Facebook double-firing (4×) — happen when four go out in one sitting. **And no double
posting.**

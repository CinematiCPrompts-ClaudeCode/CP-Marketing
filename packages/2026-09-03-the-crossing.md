## First 2 seconds — ⚠️ THE ONE THING TO FIX
The film opens on a slow push-in that starts **wide**: the woman is a small dark figure, the
bottom 40% of frame is empty cobblestone, and it takes ~2.5s to become legible. That is the
weakest frame in the film sitting in the window that decides distribution.

**Fix: trim the first 2.2 seconds.** The push-in already arrives at a strong frame — her face,
lit by the lamp, direct gaze. Start there. Costs two seconds of atmosphere, gains the hook, and
requires no re-render. Runner-up if you want a harder open: flash the café headlight frame
(~29s) for 0.5s as a cold open, then cut to the street.

## Editor notes
1. **Trim the first 2.2s.** Highest-value change in the cut.
2. **⚠️ The pistol close-up (~23s) is a distribution risk.** Firearm discharge is a restricted
   category on both TikTok and Meta, and automated classifiers do not reliably read fictional
   context. The wide shot of the man firing (~21.5s) tells the story on its own. **Cut or shorten
   the smoking-pistol insert; keep the wide and the muzzle flash.** Given this account is already
   under investigation for reach collapse, do not hand a classifier the most legible frame.
3. **No end card.** The film ends on the bottle opening — cap flying, foam. That is the payoff and
   text after it would flatten the ending. The CTA lives in the caption and a pinned comment.
   This also keeps the render clean, which is what the claims check above depends on.
4. **Café Kranzler is a real West Berlin landmark** and is legible on the sign. Worth a pinned
   comment — it is the detail Berliners will reply to, and replies are the cheapest reach signal
   available.

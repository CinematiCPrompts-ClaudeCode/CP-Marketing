# The tile fix — ordered by effect on the 1.65% (14.09.2026)

**The one number this plan exists to move.** The €10 ad bought 971 impressions and 16 taps —
**1.65% tap-through** — while tap→install ran at **25%**. At a normal 4% tap-through the same spend
buys **10 installs at €1.03**, which is exactly what was expected. The bottleneck is not keywords,
bids or budget. **It is the search-result tile, and the same tile serves organic search.**

**What a searcher currently sees** (live, "ai prompt cinemat…", 14.09):

| Tile | What it is | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | App preview video | **Poster frame is a mid-crossfade** — ghosted double wordmark over a dark UI screen. Reads as a glitch. |
| 2 | Screenshot ① "Turn a simple idea into a prompt" | Same app home screen as tile 1, on a slate background |
| 3 | Screenshot ② "Set the mood with a cinematic style" | Dense dark UI again |

Three dark, dense, near-identical rectangles, across **two different background systems** (the video
is full-bleed dark, the slides are slate `#4d4d59`). **Nothing in the first three tiles shows what
the app produces.** The product's entire value is the pictures, and none are visible.

---

## 1 — Change the poster frame · free, no release, no review

App Store Connect lets you pick the preview's poster frame. It is currently landing in a crossfade.

**Pick a frame from the first second** — the red-dress opening, fully resolved, with the wordmark
and "Prompt generator for AI image and video" settled. Verified clean in the source at **0.8–1.2s**.

This is the cheapest change available and it fixes the worst tile.

## 2 — First screenshot must show an OUTPUT · no release

**The cover built on 06.09 is still not uploaded** (5 of 10 slots used). 1284×2778, matches the set,
Gemini watermark removed, "Write better prompts / Fewer wasted credits."

Put it in slot 1 and the first screenshot stops being a screen of dense UI and becomes a picture
someone might want to make.

**★ CORRECTED 14.09.2026 — the cover CANNOT go before the video.** App previews always precede
screenshots, so **the poster frame is permanently tile 1 and nothing can displace it.** That makes
it the more important of the two fixes, not the lesser one. The cover becomes tile 2.

**Which means the red dress can only appear once.** Poster = the video's red-dress opening, so the
cover needs a different photo. Three rendered for comparison in `brand/assets/` (`var_moto.png`,
`var_espresso.png`, `var_otter.png`, side by side in `variants_thumb.jpg` at true tile size).

**Recommendation: the otter.** Three reasons, in order:
1. **It is the brightest and most distinct at tile size** — a warm orange sky next to a dark
   red-dress poster, where the motorcycle is another dark rectangle and the espresso machine is
   mid-tone. Most competitor tiles are dark; this one is not.
2. **It is the app's best-performing subject anywhere** — Instagram's best-ever post (717) and
   TikTok's 805 are both this coastal otter.
3. **★ It sets up slot 3.** Tile 2 shows the shot; tile 3 shows the same otter in five lights.
   Two tiles become one argument instead of two unrelated pictures.

**⚠️ If the otter is used, the credit line must change.** It currently reads "Image generated with
Nano Banana", and the generator behind the otter set is unverified — the Prenzlauer folder names
Seedream5, so the library is mixed. Use the generator-agnostic form: **"Image generated from a
prompt written in this app."** Accurate whatever produced it, and it still separates the prompt from
the picture, which is the job that line exists to do.

**Original note on the interaction, superseded:** if the video poster is the red dress *and* screenshot 1 is
the red dress, the first two tiles repeat again. Choose one of:
- Poster = red dress → screenshot 1 = a different strong output (the motorcycle in neon, or the
  watch under a spotlight — both high-contrast at thumbnail size)
- Poster = a mid-video output → screenshot 1 = the red-dress cover

Either works. What must not happen is two identical tiles.

## 3 — Stop the screenshots repeating the video · needs new slides

This is the user's own diagnosis and it is right. **The video already walks the steps.** Slides that
walk the same steps in a different colour are redundant — and they spend the highest-value slots
teaching a process nobody has agreed to care about yet.

**Division of labour:** the video shows *how it works*. The screenshots should show *what comes out*.

Proposed gallery, using assets that already exist:

| Slot | Shows | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Video | The nine-step walkthrough | shipped in 1.8.5 |
| 1 (screenshot) | **One output + the promise** | `brand/assets/cover.html` + chosen photo |
| 2 | **Range** — many outputs at once | the 14 marketing shots as a grid |
| 3 | **The differentiator, shown** — one scene, five lights | `Sea-Otter-5Daylight/` — dawn, sunny, Golden Hour, blue hour, night |
| 4–6 | The existing step slides, demoted | `1-8-2-{One..Five}.jpg` |

Slot 3 is the strongest unused asset in the library: identical framing, identical subject, five
genuinely different lights. It proves the core claim in one tile without a word of copy.

## 4 — One background system

The video is full-bleed dark; the slides are slate `#4d4d59`. Side by side in search they look like
two different apps. Pick one — the slate reads better at thumbnail size because the type sits on a
flat ground rather than on photography.

## Order of work

1. **Poster frame** — minutes, no release, biggest single fix
2. **Upload the cover** to slot 1 — minutes, no release
3. **Build slots 2 and 3** (range grid, five-light strip) — an hour, no release
4. **Demote the step slides** to 4–6 — minutes
5. Everything else waits for the next metadata release

**Steps 1, 2 and 4 need no new artwork and no App Review.** They can be done today.

## What this does NOT fix

The **icon**. It is in every tile and every search row, and nothing above touches it. If tap-through
is still under ~2.5% after all of the above, the icon is the remaining variable — and that is a
larger, slower piece of work. Do not start it until the cheap changes have been measured.

## How it gets measured

Organic impression→page-view, which has been running at **1.65–2%**. Watch it for two weeks after
the changes land. A second €10 ad would read it faster and cleaner — same keywords, same bids — but
only once the tiles are actually different; running it against the current set would just re-buy the
answer we already have.

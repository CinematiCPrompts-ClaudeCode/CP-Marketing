## What it actually does
You type a plain idea ("a woman waiting on a street corner"), pick a **cinematic style**, pick a **lighting/day condition**, choose **Image prompt or Video prompt**, and it outputs a detailed, filmmaker-quality prompt you copy into any AI image/video generator. It generates **prompts, not images or video.** No prompt-engineering skill needed.

## The edge (say it in every piece of content)
1. **Less guessing, fewer wasted credits.** The app writes the prompt so it lands far
   more often — you burn fewer credits on flat or wrong generator results. (Lead with
   the pain, but never *guarantee* zero waste / "first try" — the generator can still miss.)
2. **Style, lighting AND camera angle — all by choice.** Pick the look, the light, *and*
   the framing. The 5 lighting conditions: **dawn, sunny, Golden Hour, blue hour, night**
   ("Golden Hour" is the live v1.8 display name; internal id `afternoon-glow`). The 6
   camera angles (v1.8): Bird's Eye, Centered Eye-Level, Slight High, Dramatic Low,
   Close-Up, Front 3/4. Same scene re-lit or re-angled = the demo shot. ("harsh noon"/
   "overcast" don't exist — don't promise them.)
3. **Private by default.** No account, nothing leaves the device. Genuinely rare.

## Voice
Creator-to-creator. Confident, plain, a little playful. Short sentences. Lead
with the pain, then the relief. Never say "elevate," "unleash," "game-changer,"
or "in today's fast-paced world."
